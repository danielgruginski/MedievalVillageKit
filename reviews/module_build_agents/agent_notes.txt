Conventions:

IMPORTANT VAR cache gotcha: place_v caches restyled mesh copies (VAR_<hash>) forever. If a master is rebuilt after a styled instance was placed, those instances keep the old geometry. hum_refresh_variants(names) copies the new master geometry into the cached copies for the styles this module uses. Other agents' styled pieces have the same issue.

S ROOF FAMILY: fro_roof_family("S"|"M") is a context manager. It switches HALF/EAVE/RIDGE/GX (S: HALF 1.5, overhang 0.9, GX 2.1, RIDGE = roof_z(0) ≈ 2.27) and restores them in __exit__. fro_roof_slab and fro_thatch_slab are profile-driven copies of the kit roof_slab. fro_gable_end generalises gable_end to any family, with the kinds timber, planks and logs. The integrator's future roof_family infrastructure can replace this.
