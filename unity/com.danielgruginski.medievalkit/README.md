# Medieval Village Kit (Unity package)

Hand-painted modular medieval pieces (village, interiors, dungeons, caves, sewers, mines, dwarf halls) with the tools
to build levels from them: rooms typed as ASCII plans (`KitRoom`), random caves, dungeons and house floors, and chains
of maps streamed into one continuous world.

- **Building levels** (by hand or by an AI agent driving the editor): [Documentation~/LEVEL_BUILDING.md](Documentation~/LEVEL_BUILDING.md).
- **How the systems work** (the camera, the see-through, click to move, the hooks a game fills; more as they land):
  [Documentation~/MANUAL.md](Documentation~/MANUAL.md). Play From Start Level walks the start level at once.
- **Testing:** most of the kit was tested by the AI that wrote it (automated checks); the game systems were also
  played by a person, in the author's own game they came from, not on this package as such (MANUAL.md says more).
- Tools > Medieval Kit > Build All makes the materials, prefabs, level scenes and the rules asset from `Art/` and `Data/`.
- The pieces are generated by the MedievalVillageKit Blender project (github.com/danielgruginski/MedievalVillageKit);
  its `docs/UNITY_EXPORT.md` explains the export and the package's internals.
